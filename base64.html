Base64 Converter

Convert plain text to Base64 or decode a Base64 string. This tool supports UTF-8 text in modern browsers.

How to use this tool

  1. Open the tool using the button above.
  2. Enter or paste your text, or choose the available options.
  3. Generate your result and copy it when ready.

Free and browser-based

This tool is free to use and does not require an account. The tool processes your input in your browser; avoid entering sensitive information into any online service unless you understand its privacy practices.
