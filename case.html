What is a Case Converter?

A case converter is an online text formatting tool that changes the capitalization of letters without requiring you to rewrite your content. It is useful when editing documents, preparing articles, formatting headings or correcting text copied from different sources.

Available text case formats

Uppercase

Converts every letter to capital letters. For example: hello world becomes HELLO WORLD.

Lowercase

Converts every letter to lowercase. For example: HELLO WORLD becomes hello world.

Title Case

Capitalizes the words in a title-style format. For example: the quick brown fox becomes The Quick Brown Fox.

Sentence Case

Formats text like a normal sentence by capitalizing its beginning. For example: HELLO WORLD becomes Hello world.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Case Converter free?

Can I convert text to uppercase?

Yes. Select the uppercase option to change all letters in your text to capital letters.

Can I change uppercase text to lowercase?

Yes. Choose the lowercase format to convert capital letters into lowercase letters.

What is the difference between title case and sentence case?

Title case capitalizes words in a heading-style format, while sentence case generally capitalizes the beginning of a sentence and keeps the remaining words lowercase.
