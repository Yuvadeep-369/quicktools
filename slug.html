What is a URL slug?

A URL slug is the part of a web address that identifies a specific page. It usually appears after the domain name and helps describe the page's content in a readable format.

For example, a page titled "Best Free Online Writing Tools" could use this URL slug:

best-free-online-writing-tools

A clear URL slug can help visitors understand what a page is about before opening it.

Why are URL slugs important?

Descriptive URLs make website addresses easier for people to read and understand. A short, relevant slug can communicate the subject of a page and make links easier to share.

A slug generator can also save time when publishing blog posts, creating landing pages or organizing website content. Remember that a good slug alone does not guarantee better search rankings; useful content and overall website quality matter too.

Tips for creating better URL slugs

  • Keep the slug short and descriptive.
  • Use words that accurately describe the page.
  • Separate words with hyphens for readability.
  • Avoid unnecessary punctuation and symbols.
  • Avoid changing established URLs without setting up appropriate redirects.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an SEO-friendly URL slug?

An SEO-friendly URL slug is a clear and descriptive part of a web address that helps visitors understand the page's topic. It should be relevant, readable and easy to share.

Should URL slugs use uppercase or lowercase letters?

Lowercase letters are generally preferred for consistent and readable URLs. This tool generates lowercase slugs.

Should I use hyphens or underscores in a URL slug?

Hyphens are commonly used to separate words in readable URLs. They make individual words easier to identify.

Can I use this tool for blog post titles?

Yes. You can enter blog post titles, article headings, product names or other phrases to generate URL-friendly slugs.

Does a good URL slug guarantee higher Google rankings?

No. A descriptive URL can help users understand a page, but search rankings depend on many factors, including content relevance, quality and overall website performance.
