What is a Text Line Sorter?

A text line sorter is an online utility that arranges separate lines of text into a chosen order. An alphabetical sorter can organize entries from A to Z, making long lists easier to read and manage.

What can you do with this Line Sorter?

  • Sort alphabetically: Arrange lines in alphabetical order.
  • Organize lists: Put names, words and other entries into order.
  • Remove duplicates: Optionally remove repeated lines.
  • Clean up text: Make unorganized lists easier to work with.

Example of alphabetical sorting

Suppose you enter this list:

  • Orange
  • Apple
  • Banana
  • Grapes

After alphabetical sorting, the list becomes:

  • Apple
  • Banana
  • Grapes
  • Orange

Why remove duplicate lines?

Lists copied from spreadsheets, documents or websites may contain repeated entries. Removing duplicates can make a list cleaner and easier to review. It is useful when organizing contact names, keywords, product lists and other collections of text.

Who can use an online Line Sorter?

Students can organize research notes, writers can arrange lists of words, developers can sort text data, and office professionals can organize names or entries. Anyone working with lists can benefit from a simple text sorting tool.
