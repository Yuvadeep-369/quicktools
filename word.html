Free Online Word Counter

Need to know how many words are in your text? Use the free QuickTools Word Counter to count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs instantly. It is useful for students, writers, bloggers, professionals and anyone who works with text.

What does the Word Counter do?

The Word Counter helps you measure the length of written content. Whether you are preparing a school assignment, writing a blog post, editing an article or composing an email, you can quickly check your text statistics in one place.

  • Word count: Find the total number of words in your text.
  • Character count: Check how many characters your text contains.
  • Sentence count: See the number of sentences in your writing.
  • Paragraph count: Check how many paragraphs your text contains.

Who can use an online Word Counter?

This tool can help students check assignment length, writers measure article size, bloggers prepare content, and professionals review emails, reports and other documents. It is also useful whenever you need to meet a specific word or character limit.

How to use the Word Counter

  1. Click the Open Word Counter button above.
  2. Enter or paste your text into the tool.
  3. Generate the result to view your text statistics.
  4. Copy the result if you want to use it elsewhere.
